**Dataset**<br>
Data: GEM_ensemble_mean_methane_wetland_emissions.nc<br>
Contact: Xueying Yu (xyu2@albany.edu); Dylan B. Millet<br>

**Description**<br>
Wetland methane emission estimates from the GEM campaign. Estimates are derived using a multi-inversion system, including analytical sectoral inversions, GMM analytical inversions, GMM-ADJ 4D-Var inversions, and GEOS-Chem ADJ 4D-Var inversions. These inversions integrate aircraft‑based observations of methane from the GEM campaign.<br><br>
Spatial resolution: 0.25° × 0.3125°<br>
Temporal resolution: seasons<br>
Time period: 2017–2018<br>

Variables<br>
WETLAND_EMIS_CH4_ENSEMBLE_MEAN_GEM1: Wetland methane emissions in the Upper Midwest, during the GEM1 summer campaign. Ensemble mean of the multi-inversion system.<br>
WETLAND_EMIS_CH4_ENSEMBLE_MEAN_GEM2: Wetland methane emissions in the Upper Midwest, during the GEM2 winter campaign. Ensemble mean of the multi-inversion system.<br>
WETLAND_EMIS_CH4_ENSEMBLE_MEAN_GEM3: Wetland methane emissions in the Upper Midwest, during the GEM3 spring campaign. Ensemble mean of the multi-inversion system.<br>
Others: Wetland methane emissions of each ensemble member.
