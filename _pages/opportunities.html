I am actively looking for dedicated and enthusiastic PhD students, Postdoctoral researchers, and visiting students/scholars to join our team, Xueying Yu's group at the State University of New York at Albany (SUNY Albany).

Example study topics for this position include applying satellite remote sensing techniques in atmospheric chemistry, modeling the fate of greenhouse gases and air pollutants, and Bayesian inversion analyses.

Candidates seeking for PhD degree should apply through the PhD program in the Department of Atmospheric and Environmental Sciences at SUNY Albany. Please review the application instructions and mention your interest in working with our group in your application.

Interested candidates are welcome to contact Dr. Xueying Yu via email: xyu2@albany.edu. Please combine the following documents into a single PDF file and attach it to the email:

  1. CV.
  2. A one-page personal statement, including your research interests, research experience, future research ideas, and career development plans.
  3. Contact information for three recommenders (names, affiliations, and email addresses).
  4. For PhD applicants: BS and MS (if available) transcripts.
  5. For postdoctoral applicants: published papers.
